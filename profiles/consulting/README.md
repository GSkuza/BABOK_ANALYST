# Management Consulting Profile (`consulting`)

This profile supports non-IT advisory engagements: business process optimisation, digital-transformation strategy (business layer), organisation design, and C-level advisory.

## Key Principles

- **No IT Requirements:** Diagnostics and roadmaps address operating models, governance, incentives, and processes, never software delivery specs or backlog user stories.
- **Collaboration Model:**
  - **Advisor Role:** Facilitators and process architects who guide methodology rather than imposing external templates without adaptation.
  - **Client Co-Creation:** The client leadership and operational team actively co-create findings, solutions, and roadmap milestones.
  - **Procedure Ownership:** The client holds 100% post-implementation ownership of all designed procedures, tools, and operating structures.
  - **Rhythm & Governance:** Structured cadence of weekly working sessions and bi-weekly steering committee reviews.

## Stage Pipeline

- **Stage 0:** Engagement Charter & Mandate (`BC-` prefix)
- **Stage 1:** Stakeholder & Governance Mapping (including Collaboration Model and RACI)
- **Stage 2:** Current State Diagnostic & Root Cause Analysis (Operating Model Canvas, Ishikawa)
- **Stage 3:** Strategic Options & Recommendation (Weighted Evaluation Matrix)
- **Stage 4:** Target Operating Model & Change Roadmap (TOM Canvas, ADKAR)
- **Stage 5:** Risk, Governance & Change Readiness (Risk Register, Escalation Matrix)
- **Stage 6:** Business Case & Value Realization Plan (Benefits Realization Tracker, ROI/NPV)
