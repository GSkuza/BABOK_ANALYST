# RACI & Governance — Module

Use in Stage 1. Two artefacts: the engagement RACI and the steering-committee charter.

## RACI

| Activity | Sponsor | Engagement Owner | Lead Consultant | Steering Committee | Affected Functions |
|----------|---------|------------------|-----------------|--------------------|--------------------|
| Stage deliverable approval | A | R | C | I | I |
| Data and interview access | I | A | R | I | C |
| Option evaluation | C | R | R | A | C |
| Roadmap sign-off | A | R | C | C | I |
| Benefits tracking after handover | A | R | I | I | C |

R = Responsible · **A = Accountable (exactly one per row)** · C = Consulted · I = Informed
